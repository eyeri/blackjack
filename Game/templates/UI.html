Welcome to Blackjack

Beat the dealer. Get as close to 21 as possible.

How to Play

  • Players and the dealer each start with 2 cards
  • Hit to draw another card
  • Stand to end your turn
  • Go over 21 and you bust — dealer wins
  • Dealer must hit until reaching 17 or higher
  • Split pairs into two separate hands
  • Double down to double your bet for one card
  • Surrender to reclaim half your bet
